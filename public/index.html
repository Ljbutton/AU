How to play

Crewmates finish every task on the ship, or vote out all Impostors.

Impostors kill the crew, sabotage the ship and blend in. Use vents to move unseen.

  • WASD / arrows / drag to move
  • E or the USE button to work a console
  • R report a body · Q kill · F vent · TAB map
