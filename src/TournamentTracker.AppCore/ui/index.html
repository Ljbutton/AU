Help

Something went wrong? Save a support bundle and send it to the organiser: one zip with The Button's and the mod's logs, settings and versions. Bot tokens, webhooks, codes and passwords are taken out first.

The Button keeps a copy of the mod's games, links and rounds in its own folder, so reinstalling Among Us loses nothing. Restore backup puts back what's missing or older (close Among Us first).
