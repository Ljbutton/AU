Lobby voice

Each referee's Button sends the lobby's Discord voice and game sound (and their own mic only if they tick it). The voice follows the picture: the full-screen lobby, or slot 1, is heard; replays, montages and intermission are silent. Listen keeps one lobby's voice up whatever is on screen. It only goes to the stream; nothing is played back to the players. HEAR on Preview plays the lobby in Preview in your headphones (OBS's monitoring device): live, never on stream, and silent on Mute all.

Moments

Every saved clip. Click to pick (double-click to preview), drag the picked ones to change the order, then Build montage. Once a montage has played it moves to the Archive below; its clips stay here, marked USED, and can go in new montages.

Archive

Every montage that has played. Replay puts it on stream again, Rebuild makes it again from its clips (as a new montage above), Open plays the video file on this PC and Folder shows where it is. Delete removes it and its video for good (its clips stay).

Sponsors

Placements: killcam ("Kill Cam presented by" on kill replays), replay ("presented by" on every other replay), multiview (a "presented by" badge while 4 or more lobbies are on screen), montage (an opening card), standings ("Presented by" under the table), grid (empty grid tiles), break (split-screen breaks: they run their time and end by themselves; a lobby picked meanwhile goes into the break). Every appearance is logged. Verbal reads: give a sponsor "readScript" and "readEveryMinutes", "readEveryGames" or "readEveryRounds"; a reminder shows on the Live desk (only you see it), and Done logs it.

Player camera

Each host's game draws a second, close-up picture that follows one player (you pick who on the Live desk or the Stream Deck, or it follows whoever is in the action) and sends it with their game, at 960×540. Red Alert adds it to OBS as the TT Player Cam scene: the camera full screen, the whole map small in the corner. Hosts can't see it or change who it follows. On by default; it costs each host about one more video stream of upload while they send their game, so turn it off if a host's internet is weak. Replays saved while it's on get a second angle (🎥 in the replay controls, key P).

Team watch off

Your production team watches every game at once on their own PC or tablet, on the same network as this PC: the pictures, each game's state and events, how exciting it is, and who the impostors are. They send you suggestions with Show this (they show on the Live desk); they can't switch the stream. The page needs the code below. Off by default.

The first time you turn it on, Windows Firewall asks whether Red Alert may use the network: allow it on private networks only.

Updates

Red Alert never updates by itself, so nothing changes mid-broadcast. Update downloads the new version, checked against the size and SHA-256 its release lists (one that doesn't match is refused and nothing changes); it runs from the next start (Restart, or next time you open Red Alert).

Help when something goes wrong, and the rehearsal before the event
Dry-run checklist

Something went wrong? Save support bundle puts Red Alert's log, its settings, OBS's newest log and crash report, the versions, the Live desk's warnings and what the desk, OBS, the lobby voice and the lobbies' health were doing into one zip on the Desktop (RedAlert-support-….zip), and opens the folder. The administration code, passwords, keys, tokens and webhooks are taken out, so send it as it is, with a line on what went wrong and when. Hosts save theirs from The Button. The Dry-run checklist is a 30-minute rehearsal with the real hosts, OBS and the Stream Deck, to run through before the event.
