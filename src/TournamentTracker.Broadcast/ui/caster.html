Montages

Built by themselves when each game and each round ends: the moments cut to a few seconds, cropped like replays, with names in the lower third. Play puts it in the replay scene.

Moments

Every saved clip. Click to pick (double-click to preview), drag the picked ones to change the order, then Build montage. Once a montage has played it moves to the Archive below; its clips stay here, marked USED, and can go in new montages.

Archive

Every montage that has played. Replay puts it on stream again, Rebuild makes it again from its clips (as a new montage above), Open plays the video file on this PC and Folder shows where it is. Delete removes it and its video for good (its clips stay).

Sponsors

Placements: killcam ("Kill Cam presented by" on kill replays), replay ("presented by" on every other replay), multiview (a "presented by" badge while 4 or more lobbies are on screen), montage (an opening card), standings ("Presented by" under the table), grid (empty grid tiles), break (split-screen breaks; they end by themselves, and a MUST SHOW play cuts straight back). Every appearance is logged.
